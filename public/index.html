weave

Edit text together, in real time, even offline.

weave is a collaborative editor built on Fugue, a text CRDT. Every keystroke becomes an operation that merges cleanly with everyone else's, in any order and without a central lock. Disconnect, keep typing, reconnect, and everything converges.

Open the same room in two windows (or on two devices) and type in both.

Try the playground

Three simulated peers, adjustable latency and an offline switch, plus a live view of the CRDT tree. Open it →

No interleaving

When two people type at the same spot at the same time, their words stay intact instead of being shuffled together letter by letter.

Zero dependencies

The CRDT, the WebSocket protocol and the server are written from scratch in TypeScript on Node's standard library.
